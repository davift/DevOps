## Paper

### Vision Statement

- "To become the world’s most trusted company, designing smart software for a smart world.""

### Strengths

- Trust
  - While the vision uses the word "trust", in fact the organization speach and its recent aquisitions targeted **market share** as its measurement of success.
  - Although the vision should guide decisions even at the cost of short-term wins, in practice, M focused exclusively on the next new product and neglected stability, maintainability, and business continuity measure of its existing products. Thus, not aligned with its vision by any means.
- Directional
  - Since it pointed to the direction the organization wants to go, it does not rpovide numerical criteria. It gives room to adapt as markets shift (smart move).
- Memorable
  - Easy for employees to repeat and use as a decision filter.

### Weaknesses

- The vision is vague on answring:
  - "who you are" is not really mentioned,
  - "what you do" can be assumed as a software conpany,
  - "where you need to go" only points to the future.
- It goes against the principal of "The more focused your vision, the more alignment you can expect to see from your organization."

### New Vision Statement

- "We are a global family of innovators who design, deliver, and continuously improve software and services that earn trust, so every person can rely on."
- Why it works:
  - "who you are" is in "a global family of innovators",
  - "what you do" is in "design, deliver, and continuously improve software and services",
  - "where you need to go" is in "trust, so every person can rely on" and maintain the initial speach of "trust".

### Conclusion

- While I focused around the Vision, the Culture was always kept alongsite with each assessment.
  - There was a clear dichotomy between the Vision and the Culture.
  - The misalignment caused conflict on decision making and always gravitated to the short wins as a means of fullfil upper management desires, not long-term goals.
  - While inpirational words, the Vision did not resonate with the Culture and failed to "set the ambitious goals of the organization".
- I understand organization culture as the collective of peoples behaviour and intention, and not necesserily the "vague inpirational phrases" from upper leadership, if that is not what people do.
